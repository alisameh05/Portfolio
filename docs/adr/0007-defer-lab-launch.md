# Defer Lab launch

Status: accepted

The Lab / Ship Log remains part of the future product direction but is deferred from the first release. This keeps the initial public experience focused on recruiter-relevant published projects, achievements, CV access, and contact while preserving a separate domain concept for later experiments and work in progress.

**Consequences**: The first release must not expose Lab navigation or public Lab content, though the product documentation may retain its model and future scope.
