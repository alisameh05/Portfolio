# Privacy-preserving analytics

Status: accepted

ShipWithAli uses pseudonymous, aggregate-first analytics to understand portfolio performance without requiring visitor accounts or retaining unnecessary identifying detail. Geography, device, and language reporting is coarse, small samples are suppressed, engagement time is explicitly estimated, and conversion reports preserve journey context without claiming causal attribution. The product provides a clear privacy notice, honors applicable opt-out or consent requirements without blocking core use, and applies a documented retention policy with deletion or anonymization of eligible records.

**Consequences**: The dashboard may be less precise than an identity-heavy analytics system, but it remains aligned with the portfolio's evidence-led and responsible-engineering claims.
