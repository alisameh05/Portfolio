# Evidence-led public publication

Status: accepted

ShipWithAli publishes only evidence-backed content through explicit owner publication. Projects need a minimum case-study and supporting-artifact package; achievements may rely on internal owner verification when public proof is inappropriate or unavailable, but unsupported metrics and fabricated placeholders are not allowed. This protects recruiter trust while allowing incomplete but honestly labeled Lab work to remain public.

**Consequences**: Draft, Hidden, and Archived content remain outside normal public discovery, and publishing requires editorial validation rather than merely saving content.
